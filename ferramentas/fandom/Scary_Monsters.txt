== Overview==

Scary Monsters is the dinosaur-like stand of Diego Brando from Jojo's Bizarre Adventure: Steel Ball Run. The stand has a 1/5, or in other words, 20% chance to be obtained from a [https://your-bizarre-adventure.fandom.com/wiki/Rib_Cage_of_the_Saint's_Corpse Rib Cage Of the Saint's Corpse], with Worthiness 5 (to have a "guaranteed" use), of course.      

Scary Monsters is very powerful, and versatile. By default, it has a low destructive power. Its skill tree takes a lot of points, (71 points to max everything) and has a lot of upgrades. However, not all of these upgrades are needed for the stand to be versatile and worth using in combat. 

Scary Monsters is the first (and only at the moment) stand to have two different forms. One is the humanoid version, the other is the dinosaur version. In general, the humanoid version is better for applying pressure onto enemies, and the dinosaur version is better for damage output, defense and mobility. Due to all the buffs the dinosaur version provides to the user, you are unable to use any fighting-style moves when in full (dinosaur) form. 

It has the abilities any dinosaur would have - utilizing its claws as its main weapon, accompanying its tail. Despite how basic it may sound, the stand also has unique abilities, for example, putting enemies in a fossilized state, stunning them for a long time. Scary Monsters may also summon mini-dinosaur minions using a projectile. Scary Monsters has more variety than most stands and is more versatile than most other stands. Despite this, it is able to keep a fair balance between combat, mobility, and defense.  

 Total Skill Points: 71 Points

==Skills==
'''Passive - Dino-Virus:''' People hit by Scary Monsters' abilities are marked by a virus icon. When opponent has 3 virus icons and is below 5%-21% (upgradable), the will die and be converted into a dino minion similar to  [[Vampirism]]'s zombies. If the user gets below 20% HP, all virus icons will disappear from their enemies.

E - '''Barrage''' Scary Monsters performs a fast barrage of slashes that deal 1.1 damage in Base Form and 2.0 damage in Full Form.

R - '''Barrage Finisher''' Scary Monsters performs a heavy attack that does 12.5 damage in Base Form and 22.5 in Full Form.

==Trivia==
*Contrary to almost every other beat-down, excluding [[Stone Free|Stone]] [[Stone Free|Free's]] "[[Stone Free|Homerun]]", and the [[vampirism]] spec's "Oblivion Of Death", the damage of Scary Monsters' beat-down is unaffected by the health of the victim.
*Scary Monsters has the highest destructive power in the game, tied with [[D4C: Love Train]]. Scary Monsters is the first stand to have a different set of moves in an Awakening.
*It is the 3rd most expensive stand in terms of skill points, requiring 71 to learn all the moves. It is behind [[Soft & Wet|Soft and Wet]] (72) and [[The World Over Heaven|The World: Over Heaven.]] (75)
*Scary Monsters (at release) had a weird bug, where if you used "Dino Full Form" and used the command "/e sit" it would fling you extremely high
*Scary monsters is apart of the current YBA meta, due to the low windup, long range, and low end-lag of all the moves, not to mention the insane damage, (especially in the full-dino form.)
*Scary Monsters has maxed out sturdiness, when in its Full Dino Form.
*Scary Monsters’ skill tree resembles it’s full form.
