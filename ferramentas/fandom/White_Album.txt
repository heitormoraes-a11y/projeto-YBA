== Overview ==
White Album is the Stand of [[Ghiaccio (Boss)|Ghiaccio]], a side antagonist from Part 5 of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, who is a member of La Squadra Esecuzioni. White Album appears as a suit of armor the user wears, it does this by freezing the moisture around the user, forming an armor of ice. White Album has a very potent power called Cryokinesis that is able to freeze things in a matter of seconds and drop temperatures to -100°C with ease.

White Album has some good close-ranged comboing moves that can be used on top of each other for high-damaging combos (keep in mind Ice Swipe can't be confirmed by Ice Punch or Ice Sweeps). It also has two very good utility moves, along with a large hitbox move in the form of flash freeze, which is practically undodgable. 

White Album has a passive, ''Ice Armor'' which reduces damage taken by up to 20%, this allows White Album to be used offensively at the front line, being able to take more damage and act as a tank. The passive, however, is ignored by Beatdowns and [[The World Over Heaven]].

''Gently Weeps'' is a valuable asset. It passively slows down everyone nearby the user, allowing for an easier time catching a target. Furthermore, an overlooked ability of Gently Weeps is that it will automatically redirect ranged attacks to whoever casted it. This makes [[Pesci (Boss)|Pesci]] a much easier boss while also being able to trivialize zoning dependent stands such as [[The World (Alternate Universe)]] or [[Six Pistols]] as it requires the Stand to engage in melee combat, which is what it is weaker at.

''Ice Skating'' gives the Stand some mobility, it allows the user to outrun other players with maxed out agility and makes it the third fastest Stand in the game, with only [[Scary Monsters]] and [[Made in Heaven]] being able to out speed it. Even then, White Album rivals Scary Monster's speed and can slow down others with Gently Weeps. It can also be tapped to have a very low cooldown and act like a faster, higher-ranged dash.

Typically, [[Boxing]] is used with White Album mainly for the extra defense being able to make White Album into even more of a tank. It also adds close range stun in the form of Knuckle Sandwich and Liver Shot, allowing for combo starting or extension. Liver shot especially is important as it confirms ice swipe.

White album is an excellent choice for [[Steel Ball Run]], whether solo or in a group. The speed of ice skating, the slow/reflection of gently weeps, and the vertical maneuverability afforded by flash freeze allow the user to escape from and catch escaping enemies.

The bleed on Cryokinesis is very useful for negating regeneration overall, whether the enemy be a vampire or not. Since White Album has a low destructive power, keeping a constant source of damage on the enemy helps when it's moves are on cooldown.

==Trivia==

*White Album, [[Anubis]], and [[Beach Boy]]  are currently the only stands to belong to a tertiary antagonist.
*If you equip White Album while being slowed down, you will completely ignore the effect.
*This Stand used to be able to float in the air using Ice Skate.
*White Album's old model was based on the manga.
*Frozone is based on The Incredibles character [https://the-incredibles.fandom.com/wiki/Frozone Frozone]. Similar to White Album, he also possesses abilities related to ice as well as a super suit. Altough White Album's armor is ice manipulated by him to look like a suit of armour while Frostzone's is a fully man-made suit.
*Before you could get Frozone or Vanilla Ice Cream, White Album alongside [[Cream]] was known for destroying lucky arrow rolls. With no shiny variant available for the stands, getting it from the lucky arrow would make the arrow's 90% calculation invalid and would automatically give you the generic version of the stand.  
*White Album can fly on horses in Steel Ball Run when it uses Ice Skate.  
*The voiceline for White Album's Stand Barrage Finisher is a direct translation in Japanese to "dumbass."
*During the 2026 April Fools Update, '''White Album'''<nowiki/>'s '''Gently Weeps''' and '''Ice Skates''' were buffed. '''Ice Skates''' had its cooldown cut in half, while '''Gently Weeps''' reflect damage was exponentially increased, the quote in the v1337 Update logs funnily mentioned "yo do NOT shoot white album players when they're using gently weeps...".
*White Album does NOT protect Vampires from the <nowiki/>sun.
