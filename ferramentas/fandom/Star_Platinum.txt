==Overview==
Star Platinum is a close-range [[Stands|stand]] wielded by Jotaro Kujo, who is the protagonist of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3: Stardust Crusaders. This stand does a good job of filling up that role of a protagonist's stand due to it's above-average destructive power and it's almost at the speed of light attack speed. 

Star Platinum can be upgraded to [[Star Platinum: The World]]. [[Star Platinum: The World]] has more overall damage and has better AoE attacks, which allows it to be used more easily to win fights that are not 1 on 1 combat. Star Platinum has access to Star Finger and Inhale, two powerful stun tools which allow for easier damage output and better combos.

Star Platinum's best move is probably Star Finger, as it has a very long range and works well as both a combo starter and extender with it's very good stun time. It also boasts a short cooldown compared to most current stun moves like Fossilization from [[Scary Monsters]] and Yo Angelo from [[Crazy Diamond]].

==Skills==
LMB - '''Punch:''' This short move with a short cooldown deals 8.9 damage at max destructive power and can be combo'd 5 times, ultimately dealing 44.5 damage if all hit. 

RMB - '''Heavy punch:''' Double tapping initiates a block breaking move with animation similar to finisher that deal 12.1 (15.1 in rage mode) 

E - '''Barrage:'''  Star Platinum performs an astoundingly fast flurry of punches that deals small damage per hit. The sound effect for the barrage has been changed. (1.5 at max Destructive Power per hit) 1.8 with rage mode.

R - '''Barrage Finisher:''' Star Platinum throws out a heavy punch that ragdolls the victim. Breaks block but CAN be perfectly blocked. (16.1 (22.1 in Rage Mode) damage max.)

T - '''Star Finger:''' Star Platinum extends its finger and stabs the target in the eyes, screaming (STAR (then hits) FINGER!), stunning for 2 seconds and blinding the target for 5 seconds, if the move hits, it would make the victim's screen red and blurry. Which is decent for starting combos but can very often get blocked. (15 damage max) This move is similar to Eye Gouge from the pre-reworked [[Boxing]], but it has more range and damage and slightly less stun. This is Star Platinum's only range option. (12 Seconds Cooldown) 

Y - '''Ora Kicks:''' Star Platinum performs 6 kicks that deal low damage per hit, then spins in place to perform a powerful elbow strike, knocking the enemy away. (The kicks deal 1.6, and the final kick deals 12.9 at max Destructive Power. Note that the final kick is a true stand crashing true block break, meaning it cannot be parried or blocked.) This move can be perfect blocked at the first kick of the barrage. (18 seconds cooldown)

G - '''Inhale:''' Once activated, Star Platinum inhales the air so powerfully that it causes people near you to be drawn in front of the user.  This move has massive range and can almost drag anyone far away to you. However, you can not use any attack or abilities while using inhale, your enemies also can not do anything while being dragged by inhale beside using barrage. This move is good to use against runners and catching players while they're stand crashed for a free barrage. Inhale does nothing in stopped time. (20 seconds cooldown)

Z - '''Time Skip: '''Teleports the user wherever their mouse cursor is with limited range (This move can't be used on time stop and time stop becomes on cooldown for 3 seconds). Rage Mode will extend radius of Time Skip. The only way to travel vertically is to jump and aim above.

H - '''[[Rage Mode]]:''' When Rage Meter is full you can activate Rage Mode which gives Star Platinum a 25% damage boost and a 20% damage reduction, similar to [[Spin|Spin's]] Golden Skin. When fully upgraded, Rage Mode lasts for 50 seconds.

J - '''[[Time Stop|The World]]:''' Star Platinum stops time for a short amount of time. The duration for the time stop is 5 second at base, can be upgraded to max at 8 second. Pressing the button again cancels it (70 seconds cooldown)

Note 1: Upon stopping your m1 combos , you will be given significant endlag for the next m1 combo. Damage cap is 1/3 total HP of opponent and time stop no longer required rage.

Note 2: Time stop upgrade now also affect your Time Stop Resistance duration.

X - '''Stand Jump:''' You and Star Platinum jump high into the air. The jump's height varies depending on the number of upgrades you have, and will launch you twice as far during a Time Stop thanks to reduced gravity.

C - '''Platinum Slam:''' Star Platinum smashes the ground, dealing AoE damage and incredible knockback, stand-crashing anyone hit. This does around 30.2 damage and has a 20-second cooldown. This move Breaks block but is Perfect Parriable.

==Trivia==

*Star Platinum currently has the most skins for a single stand, 9, the seconds Being 8 With [[The World]], and [[Six Pistols]]
*Star Finger is usually a combo starter. Boxing used to be able to stack it's blindness with Eye Gouge to Star Finger.
*The Inhale move was based on an episode in which [https://jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Jotaro_Kujo Jotaro] used Star Platinum to inhale [https://jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Enya_the_Hag Enyaba's] stand, [https://jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Justice Justice].
*Star Finger was only used twice in the Manga (three times within the Anime) due to it not being strong enough and used as a last resort.
*This Stand has been renamed "Platinum Sun" in-game in order to avoid [[YBA Renames|copyright issues]]. This was overturned later.
*Star Platinum's SFX for Rage Mode is a sped up version of [https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Haseldan Haseldan's] Bloodline ability from Rogue Lineage.
*Star Platinum's Summon voice line changed to: "Star Platinum"
*Star Platinum's skin, Mustard Platinum, is taken from when Star Platinum fought [[Hierophant Green]].
*Star Platinum's skin, Charmy Green, is taken from Jota Kujo's Stand with the same name from a series that has been cancelled.
*Star Platinum's skin, Jaguar Platinum, is taken from the fight between Jotaro and his Star Platinum and [https://jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Terence_T._D%27Arby Telence T. D'Arby] and his [https://jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Atum Atum].
* Star Platinum so far has 8 skins, 1 being permanent unobtainable, and 1 being seasonal limited and others being all-time obtainable.
*Star Platinum, [[The World]], Pre-rework [[Crazy Diamond]] and [[The World (Alternate Universe)]] all share similar skill tree diagrams, likely due to the way they all have a Rage Mode (hypothesis). This is different now with the Crazy Diamond rework, where it's skill tree is in the shape of two hearts.
*Star Platinum is the same type of stand as [[The World]], explaining its incredible speed, strength and precision. This is how Jotaro is also able to stop time, but he is only able to for about 1-2 seconds in Part 3-4. Star Platinum's Stand stats are actually better than The World's stats, having an A in all except it's range, which is C.
*There is a custom Admin Stand of this stand named [[Paragon Platinum|Paragon Platinum.]]
* The final hit animation of Star Platinum and Star Platinum: The World's Ora Kicks has the stand attack with their elbow instead of another kick. This is probably inspired by the the anime where Star Platinum countered The World's chop by turning around and intercepting the attack with it's elbow in this scene [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i-L0Gs2whvc here].
* Star Platinum has the most model reworks in the entire game.
