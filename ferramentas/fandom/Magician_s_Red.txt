==Overview==
Magician's Red appears as a humanoid figure with a bird-like head. It has a heavily muscular upper body and its feathered legs are sometimes covered in burning flames. Its arms have claws instead of nails and it wears dark bracelets on both of its wrists. Originally, the Stand was barefooted, but during Avdol's return, it wore Arabian shoes.
This stand is typically paired with [[Hamon]] or [[Sword-Style]] due to the increased damage and defense from the former and the combo potential of the latter. 

Magician's Red is the stand of Muhammad Avdol, a core ally of Part 3 of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. It is based off of the Tarot card, The Magician and its abilities are based off of Pyrokinesis. It's a real powerhouse with it's abilities to manipulate fire and deal immense damage with a wide variety of powerful attacks. The only weakness of this stand is it's slow and weak barrage, as well as the fact that that the majority of it's moves can be blocked, the one exception being Crossfire Hurricane and Stand Barrage Finisher. Specs can cover this weakness.
 Total Skill Points: 50 Points

==Trivia ==

*Magician's Red is the very first Stand fully revealed in the entire series, with its face showing even before Star Platinum's. Coincidentally, [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Magician_(Tarot_card) Magician], which is Magician's Red's namesake, is the first card of the Major Arcana.
*Magician's Red was given a bird-like head so that the the heroes' silhouettes wouldn't look too similar to one another when they're side by side.
*This Stand has been renamed "Magician's Ember" in-game in order to avoid [[YBA Renames|copyright issues]].
*In the anime, Cross Fire Hurricane starts out as one, and can be multiplied into as many as Avdol wants. However, in-game you only release 3.
*The move "Bakugo" is named after a name of Japanese descent that means "explosion" or "powerful".
*Different from other JoJo fighting games, the moves E and R will not burn the enemy.
*Magician's Red was reworked on June 6th, 2021.
*Magician's Red's third skin is based off [https://project-jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Star-Spangled_Banner Star Spangled Banner] from [https://www.roblox.com/games/2295122555/Project-JoJo Project JoJo], one of the first Roblox JoJo games that is now outdated and no longer receiving updates.[https://www.roblox.com/games/2295122555/Project-JoJo]<br />
