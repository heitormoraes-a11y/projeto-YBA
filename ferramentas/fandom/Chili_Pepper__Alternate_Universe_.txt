== Overview==
Chili Pepper (Alternate Universe) is an evolved non-canon derivation and an evolution of the stand [[Red Hot Chili Pepper]] from the anime Jojo's Bizarre Adventure: Diamond Is Unbreakable. It features a white robe over it's blue body and golden armor, blue, electricity-like particles surrounding it when visible. Its ability revolves around manipulating electricity and converting it to attacks. Similar to its predecessor stand, it allows the user to play a guitar in order to fill a meter and spend it to get heightened power and stronger effects.
 Total Skill Points: 60 Points

==== Obtainment ====
To obtain '''Chili Pepper (Alternate Universe)''', you must: 

# Obtain the original version, [[Red Hot Chili Pepper]]. (4% chance when using a [[Mysterious Arrow]])
# Use a [[Heart of the Saint's Corpse]] while having '''Worthiness V'''.

'''If it is used without Worthiness V and at least Prestige I, you will turn into stone and your heart will be removed from your inventory.'''

==Skills==
LMB - '''Punch:''' A basic 5-hit combo dealing a total of 37.5 damage (7.5 damage per hit).

E - '''Barrage:''' A rapid sequence of punches, dealing 1.3 damage per punch, 52 damage in total. ''(Cooldown: 7s)''

R - '''Barrage Finisher:''' A heavy punch that deals 12 damage. ''(Cooldown: 7s)''

G - '''Electric Guitar:''' The User begins playing an Electric Guitar, filling up the Electric Charge bar of the user. ''(Cancellable, 2s)''

T - '''Thunderbolt:''' Fires a medium-ranged projectile that deals, 16 damage when it is uncharged. When charged it deals 25 damage also will deal 10 chip damage if the enemy is blocking. ''(Cancellable, Partially-Blockable)''

Y - '''Pinky Slash:''' Does a close-ranged slash, this move deals 20 damage regardless of charge, applying bleed when it's charged, dealing 15 hp over 8 seconds. ''(Cancellable, Guard-Break, 16s)''

X - '''Godspeed Teleport:''' Allows the user to dash instantly to the direction of the key last pressed, if an entity is in the way, it will deal 6.7 damage. If space is the last key they've pressed, the user will be teleported upwards. Upgraded versions provide up to 3 charges. ''(Cancellable, Blockable, 12s max upg.)''

C - '''Lightning Drop:''' The User performs a downward slam. If the user is on the ground, it will deal 12 damage while giving the user a significantly long endlag. If the user is airborne, the drop will be a slam instead and deal upwards of 30 damage. This move is more versatile with the help of the ''Teleport'' skill and is blockable unless you have any Electric Charge. ''(Cancellable, Blockable/Bypass, 18s)''

Z - '''Lightning Elbow:''' The User charges forward, dealing a blockable elbow which ragdolls the victim dealing 12.5 damage when the user has no charge. While the user has charge and happens to land this move, the move becomes a combo starter/extender adding ragdoll stun on get up to the victim and deals 20 damage, however, it is easy to block because of its obvious start-up. ''(Cancellable, Blockable, 20s)''

==Tips & Tricks ==

* '''Godspeed Teleport''' and '''Lightning Drop''' work well for baiting barrages and evading.
* Use '''Charged Lightning Elbow''' as a combo starter/extender, but stay unpredictable due to its blockable nature.
* Do not try to use moves during M1 trades since all moves are cancellable.
* It's recommended to get the +2 Godspeed Capacitor dash upgrades.
* '''Godspeed Teleport'''s are powerful combo starters that can be used to stun and catch up to people.
* '''Thunderbolt''' is one of the best moves of the stand and should be used frequently.
* Guitarist upgrades are important as Electric Charge is the key to winning with this stand.
